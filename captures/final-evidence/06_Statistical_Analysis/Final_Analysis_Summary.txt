Methodology and limitations:
Workload latency uses Received timestamps and Maildir modification times.
Delivery latency estimates have limited timestamp precision.
Workload error bars use sample SD of the three run averages.
SMTP and STARTTLS error bars use individual measurement sample SD.
Error bars extending below zero are clipped at zero.
2% packet loss was configured in the forward direction.
1 Mbps bandwidth restriction was configured in both directions.
Test 02 was recovered from an independent repeated baseline run.
Test 10 had a prolonged delivery; confirm whether manual queue flush was used.
Negative SPF and DKIM functional cases each have one verified sample.
DKIM failure detection does not necessarily imply rejection.
Supplementary SMTP probes are excluded from workload authentication rates.
